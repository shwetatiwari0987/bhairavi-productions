Who we are

About Bhairavi Productions

Bhairavi Productions started with a simple idea — devotion deserves the same craft as any big-budget film. We bring cinematic direction, sound design and music production together, so every bhajan and every festival film feels as good as it looks.

Our work lives first on Instagram, where festival specials, studio sessions and new releases reach a growing community — built one honest production at a time, not on paid promotion.

What we believe

Sound and picture should be planned together from day one, not fixed separately in post.

How we work

Small, hands-on crew — direction, recording and editing stay in-house from brief to release.

Who we work with

Artists, temples and event organisers who want devotional content made with real production value.

What we do

Two crafts, one story

Every project moves between the camera and the studio — we handle both, so the sound and the image are built together, not stitched apart.

Video Production

From concept to final cut — music videos, devotional shorts and festival films shot and edited end to end.

  • Music video direction & shoot
  • Devotional & festival films
  • Colour grading and edit

Music Production

Original bhajans and songs — composed, recorded and mixed in-house with vocalists and session musicians.

  • Composition & arrangement
  • Studio recording
  • Mixing & mastering

Devotional Content

Festival specials and spiritual storytelling made for social platforms, temples and events.

  • Janmashtami & festival specials
  • Reels & social-first cuts
  • Event & live coverage

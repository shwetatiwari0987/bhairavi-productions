About Bhairavi Productions

A digital media and production house that makes content look and sound the way it should.

Bhairavi Productions is a digital media and production house. We create music videos, ad films, songs, reels, photography and complete social media content for artists, brands, businesses and events.

Whether it is a devotional song, a wedding film, a brand story or a viral reel - we plan the sound and the picture together, so everything we make looks and feels like one story.

Our small, hands-on team handles direction, shooting, recording and editing in-house, from the first idea to the final release.
